# Problem Statement
Local dairy shops often manage their daily operations using notebooks.

They manually record:
- Customer details
- Daily milk quantity
- Milk rates
- Monthly payments

This creates several problems:
- Manual calculations take time
- Chances of billing mistakes
- Difficult to track pending payments
- Searching old records is difficult
- No organized customer history

The aim is to create a simple digital system that reduces manual work and improves accuracy for dairy owners.
